# Verification log: cross-provider leg, 2026-11-10

## How this leg ran

This is the record behind the `last-verified: 2026-11-10` dates on the analysis documents, so that a date in the frontmatter points at a check someone can repeat rather than standing on its own. For each shipped analysis document (the template and `analysis/stubs/` excluded) I picked the three most consequential measured or numeric claims, sent them in one prompt per document to Gemini 3.8 Flash (High) through the Antigravity CLI in print mode, with web search on and an instruction to check each claim against the source the document cites, and then resolved every answer against the primary myself. Twenty calls ran in all: seventeen claim prompts, two re-asks on answers that disagreed with the primary, and one tool check at the start. Gemini's answers are treated as a triage signal at Tier C until the primary is read, because a second model agreeing with a first is one more opinion rather than a measurement, and on two claims below it was the second model that was wrong.

The leg ran in two modes, and the table says which one each row used.

- Public mode: the claim rests on a public primary (a standard, statute, paper, vendor page or public dataset), Gemini checked it directly, and I re-read the primary where it could be fetched.
- Lab mode: the claim rests on a result in sdw-lab-benchmarks on branch `hub/ai-research-2026-11-09`, which is not yet public, so Gemini could check only the public parts (vendor documentation of the behaviour, specification text, codec defaults) and the arithmetic, and the verdict on the number itself comes from my reading of the cited `RESULTS.md` at the branch head, commit `91e44df`. A Gemini "confirmed" in this mode is never a verdict on the lab number.

Verdict vocabulary: "holds" means the primary says what the document says; "holds, Gemini wrong" means Gemini disputed it and the primary sided with the document (Gemini retracted on a re-ask in both such cases); "fixed" means the document was corrected in place today; "Gemini only" means Gemini confirmed it and I could not fetch the primary myself, so the row carries one model's check and should be re-read by a human before release.

What this leg backs: the `last-verified` date on the seventeen documents in the table, for the claims listed, with the "Gemini only" rows as the weaker part of that backing. What it does not back: `evidence-tiers.md`, which carries no measured or numeric claim and was out of scope for this leg, so its date still rests on the drafting session alone; and any claim not listed here, which keeps whatever check its drafting session gave it.

Two citation-hygiene notes came out of the run. The `coding-agent-hooks-and-policy.md` rows cite `t9-pretooluse-hook-enforcement/RESULTS.md:20-22` at a pending hash, and at `91e44df` the headline those rows quote sits at lines 19-23 (the precedence finding at 24-32 is unchanged and the E1c cell is the table row at line 100); the hash placeholders are left for the lab-landing step to replace. Gemini's own citation for the Aghili paper gave an arXiv identifier that resolves to an unrelated signal-processing paper, while the DOI the document cites, 10.1145/3715779, resolves through Crossref to the right paper, so the document's citation stands and the verifier's did not.
